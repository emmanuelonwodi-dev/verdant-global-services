Delivery Capabilities

Any Mode.
Any Destination.

Verdant manages freight across all modes and routes. We select the right transport method based on your urgency, cargo weight and dimensions, origin, and destination — not the easiest option.

For time-critical shipments, we have access to air freight consolidation and charter options. For bulk or heavy cargo, we coordinate sea freight and heavy-lift logistics. For last-mile delivery to remote or restricted sites, we work with specialized ground handlers.

Air Freight

Express, standard, and charter options. Fastest route for critical, time-sensitive parts under 500kg.

Ocean Freight

Full container load (FCL) and less-than-container load (LCL) for heavy machinery and bulk shipments.

Road & Ground

Flatbed, specialized heavy transport, and final delivery to rig sites, farms, mines, and construction projects.

Customs & Trade Compliance

We Handle the Paperwork. All of It.

International industrial procurement generates a significant amount of customs and compliance documentation. Getting it wrong means delays, demurrage charges, or seizure of goods. Getting it right the first time requires experience.

Verdant manages the full documentation lifecycle for international shipments, including HS code classification, export license screening, import duty calculation, and customs broker coordination in destination countries.

Export Documentation

Commercial invoices, certificates of origin, packing lists, export declarations, and EEI filings.

Import Clearance

Customs broker coordination, duty payment, and post-clearance documentation in destination markets.

Dangerous Goods

IATA and IMDG compliant handling and documentation for regulated materials when required.

Offshore & Remote Delivery

We Go Where Your Operation Is

Industrial operations don't always run from convenient locations. Neither does ours.

OFFSHORE

Platform Delivery

Coordination with offshore logistics operators for helicopter and supply boat delivery to platforms, FPSOs, and drilling vessels. We understand the scheduling requirements and documentation for offshore customs zones.

REMOTE

Remote Site Delivery

Mining sites, remote farms, and land rig locations require specialized last-mile logistics. We work with ground handlers experienced in remote and access-restricted destinations.

PROJECT

Project Cargo

For large equipment and multi-item project orders, we provide project cargo management including heavy-lift coordination, oversize permitting, and phased delivery scheduling.
